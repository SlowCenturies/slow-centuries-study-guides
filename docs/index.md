# Slow Centuries Study Guides

<div class="timeline-strip"></div>

Free, classroom-ready guides for the **Slow Centuries** YouTube series. Each episode watches one system take shape over thousands of years. Each guide gives you a summary, a timeline, key terms, an activity, discussion questions and links to the real objects in museums and archives.

## Episodes

| Episode | Topics | Guide |
|---|---|---|
| **Timelapse of Algorithms** | Babylonian math, Euclid, al-Khwarizmi, Lovelace, Turing, ENIAC, search and machine learning | [Open the guide](algorithms/index.md) · [PDF](pdf/timelapse-of-algorithms.pdf) |
| **Timelapse of Money** | Clay tablets, the first coins, paper money, banks, the gold standard, Bretton Woods, cards and Bitcoin | [Open the guide](money/index.md) · [PDF](pdf/timelapse-of-money.pdf) |
| **Timelapse of Encryption** | The scytale, Caesar, al-Kindi, Mary Queen of Scots, Enigma, Bletchley Park, public keys and quantum | [Open the guide](encryption/index.md) · [PDF](pdf/timelapse-of-encryption.pdf) |
